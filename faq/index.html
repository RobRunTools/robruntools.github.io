Wipes and cash

When does the server wipe, and what do I lose?

Every server (the game calls them states) gets raided 7 days after it opened. Kefir calls it the Federal Raid. There's no single wipe day for everyone. To see where yours is, look at Server lifetime in the main menu, bottom right above RUN. It counts up from when the server opened, so 5D 2H means you have about 1 day 22 hours left. The LIFETIME column in State Selection shows the same number and turns red in the last day.

When the raid hits, you get sent to prison and that server closes. You escape from your cell, pick a new state and start the next 7 days there. One fresh server opens every day in each region, EU at 5 PM UTC and East US at 8 PM New York time (7 PM Central), and extra ones open whenever the others fill up. Kefir says 7 days is the length for now and could change.

You lose all your cash, including what's in the bank, plus items, vehicles, houses, land, your unlocks, clan spending and bribes. All of it gets turned into jail time, and more jail time raises your Authority, which unlocks shipments and tattoos. Since November 17 there's a cap on how much jail time you can earn. Kefir didn't give numbers, but the game's own data says 40 years a day and 1,200 years in total.

You keep cosmetics, B-Coins, your look and name, tutorial progress, settings and your total jail time.

Bottom line: spend before the wipe. Money in the bank on wipe day is gone. Put your Server lifetime into the wipe clock on the home page and it counts down to your raid and warns you on the last day.

How do I keep my cash from getting stolen?

Open an account at Looty Bay Bank. It costs $5,000, and you have to walk in as a Civilian, not with your mask on. Then put your dirty cash in the Laundering Desk at your base. It takes a while, and the clean money goes straight to your bank where nobody can take it.

If you pull it back out at an ATM, it's stealable again. Before you have a base, Lenny will launder for you, but he takes a cut. Your own desk is free. If you use Lenny, put his fee in the split calculator so your numbers are real.

Your gear also loses durability every time you die, and there's no way to repair it yet. Kefir says repairs are planned.

Taking your mask off doesn't clear Criminal status. You have to actually leave Looty Bay.

What is Notoriety?

Hit places back to back in the same trip into the city and your Notoriety goes up. Higher Notoriety gets you more from the Fence and the Smuggler, up to about 50% more at the top. That's the low-to-high range on the price check. The catch is that your bag gets bigger and cops and other players start hunting you.

It resets when you leave Looty Bay. Solo players start at Notoriety level 4 and get bonuses on selling and loot. Duos also start higher than full crews. Kefir says it's also looking at separate matchmaking for solo players in the city.

Selling

Where should I sell my loot?
  • Fence: the dollar signs on the map. Buys stolen items. Grab your cash off the counter right after you sell, because money left there can be stolen.
  • Smuggler (Cargo Exchange): buys cargo. Bigger deliveries pay better, and each one adds a point to a Cargo Sheet that you can sell once it's full.
  • Sales Center: one in each district. Buys everything in your trunk, and the price depends on the district's level. You can only trade there if you rent a plot in that district.

Black Market stuff sells back for a lot less than you paid. A Longhorn Wild costs $4.5M and the fence gives you about $1.8M for it, so don't buy things to flip them.

Should I sell cargo or open it?

Open it. Since the November 5 patch, opening cargo at a workbench in your base pays more than selling it as is. Contraband from Lenny is worth next to nothing until you repack it on a Contraband Workbench, and since November 20 the game warns you if you try to buy contraband without a home location.

Two ways to lose it on the way: drawing a weapon while you're carrying cargo makes you drop it, and switching cars dumps whatever was in the old trunk on the ground.

Unlocks and shopping

How do unlocks work?

Press H to open Larry's Shipments menu. Since November 17 you can also open it from Lenny's menu. Each item costs money to unlock, and then you buy it on the Black Market. Some items also need your base in a settlement of a certain level, or a minimum amount of jail time.

Check the requirements before you save up, because more cash won't get you past a settlement lock. Example: the Longhorn Wild costs $500k to unlock after the Royal E, needs a level 3 settlement, and then costs $4.5M on the Black Market.

Kefir's wipe post lists your unlocked "skills and tech" among the things the feds take, so plan on starting the tree over every wipe. Jail time carries over, though, so the jail-time locks get easier each week.

The unlock tree adds all of this up for you, path and all.

Black Market or city stores?

Use the Black Market, through Lenny or your base laptop. City stores charge about three times as much. A lot of Black Market stock has to be unlocked in the Shipments tree first, and you can't buy from vendors while your base is being raided.

Cops

Playing cop: how do I join and what does it pay?

Joining: talk to the officer at the Police Station when a slot is open. Since November 5 there can only be 4 cops in the city at once, so you might have to wait, and since November 20 the game checks for a free spot before it lets you pick your gear. You get a free uniform, weapons and a car, and the department holds your own gear until you clock out. If you're in a party, everyone in Looty Bay has to play the same role. On a fresh server, patrol cops only get pistols for the first 30 minutes.

Pay: Kefir says cops earn a percentage of the value of the loot they confiscate, so a rich robber pays far more than a broke one.

  • Arrests pay more than kills, and killing someone who surrendered pays nothing.
  • Turn your siren on and use the Surrender Order for bonuses.
  • There's an earnings cap per shift.
  • Since November 5, a quarter of your pay comes as keys, badges and gear, and the rest is cash.
  • Badges (Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain) get you better gear. They're single-use, so if you die on duty you need a new one.

Arrested criminals can offer you a bribe out of their bank account, and taking it is your call.

Launch guides listed these amounts. Kefir raised cop pay on November 5 without posting new numbers, so treat them as a minimum:

Servers and crew

How do I switch servers or find my crew's server?

Since November 20, online friends show up in the server list, so you can see which server your crew is on. Your state ID is at the bottom of the settings screen. Send it to your crew and they can type it into the search at the top of State Selection.

To switch, you still have to go back to prison: use Surrender to Feds (Settings, Other tab), then escape from your cell and pick the new state. You lose everything you built on the old one. Kefir says an easier way to pick a server is coming.

If you change regions, your progress and Battle Pass points now come with you (since November 17). There are only EU and East US servers for now. Kefir says no new regions are coming in the next month, and Asia or Oceania won't happen before the end of 2026.

How big can my crew be?

Parties max out at 3, and Kefir isn't planning to raise that, because bigger parties meant fewer random run-ins in testing. Clans can have up to 100 people.

Quick ones

Why can't I type in general chat or talk on voice?

Only players renting a settlement plot can post in general chat. Since November 17, voice chat in prison and at Lenny's Motel only works if you own a land plot or have jail time on your record.

How do I change my name?

Talk to the Tattoo Artist in prison. There's a profanity filter on names now, so keep it clean.

Where did the smoke grenades go?

Kefir pulled them from the stores on November 11 because cheaters were abusing them. Since November 17 the Smoke Grenade's unlock is switched off too, so it's gone from the unlock tree and the Frag Grenade now comes straight after the Flash Grenade. Kefir said they'd still drop as loot, just a lot less often.

Is there anything I shouldn't sell?

Yes. Keys open safes, lottery tickets can pay out big, and a jammer is how you steal the Buletti from the Grand Hotel. Kefir says it's working on making those harder to sell by accident.
