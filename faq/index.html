FAQ

Quick answers to the stuff people keep asking. Last checked November 13, 2026, so things may have changed since the latest patch.

When does the server wipe, and what do I lose?

Every 7 days the Federal Raid resets the server. Your plot, base, weapons and cash are gone, and that includes money in the bank.

You keep your cosmetics, your name, your settings and tutorial progress, and your accumulated jail time. The stuff that gets wiped is converted into jail time and authority, so it isn't a total loss.

Bottom line: spend before the wipe. A big purchase the night before a reset is mostly wasted.

How do I keep my cash from getting stolen?

Open an account at Looty Bay Bank. You have to walk in as a Civilian, not with your mask on. Then launder your dirty cash at the Laundering Desk in your base. Once it's in the bank, nobody can take it.

If you pull it back out at an ATM, it's stealable again. Before you have a house, Lenny will launder for you, but he takes a cut. If you use him, put his fee in the split calculator so your numbers are real.

What is Notoriety?

Hit places back to back in the same trip into the city and your Notoriety goes up. Higher Notoriety means the fence pays you more, but your bag gets bigger and cops and other players start hunting you.

It resets when you leave Looty Bay. Solo players and duos start with higher Notoriety than full crews.

Fence van or Sales Center?

Fence vans are the dollar signs on the map. They're quick and close. Grab your cash off the counter after you sell, because money left there can be stolen.

Sales Centers pay better, but you need a plot in that district to use one.

Black Market or city stores?

Use the Black Market, through Lenny or your base laptop. City stores charge about three times as much. A lot of Black Market stock has to be unlocked in the Shipments tree first, and you can't buy from vendors while your base is being raided.

How do unlocks work?

Press H to open Larry's Shipments menu. Each item costs money to unlock, and then you buy it on the Black Market. Some items also need your base in a settlement of a certain level, or a minimum amount of jail time.

Check the requirements before you save up, because more cash won't get you past a settlement lock. Example: the Longhorn Wild costs $500k to unlock after the Royal E, needs a level 3 settlement, and then costs $4.5M on the Black Market.

The unlock tree adds all of this up for you, path and all.
